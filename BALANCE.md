# Damage expectation worksheet

Generated by `npm run balance`. Values are expected damage per turn using two offensive actions and a two-handed d12 weapon. See PLAYTEST.md for assumptions and exclusions. Target ACs are explicit scenario inputs, not a complete creature model. Fundamental weapon runes enter at levels 2/4/10/12/16/19; an apex attribute increase is assumed at 17. No property runes, off-guard, feats, buffs, resistances, or protective reactions are included.

The normal martial baseline is two ordinary Strikes at normal MAP. Fighter has its higher proficiency and corresponding specialization, but no offensive feats. Sustainable Gunbreaker cycles Keen Edge → Brutal Shell → Solid Barrel → Burst Strike over two turns, with no cartridges spent on protection. The burst window is Burst Strike + Strike before level 7, then the three-turn Gnashing sequence with Continuation on a hit or a normal second Strike on a miss. These are illustrative routines, not optimized builds or a balance verdict.

The Gunbreaker burst column cannot be sustained: Gnashing Fang has a one-minute frequency and only one step can be used per turn. Lost reach or an intervening action removes the follow-up. Holding a cartridge for Heart of Stone reduces available offense. The basic cycle is deliberately a smaller improvement than the burst window. Compare full four-round encounters and ally damage prevention before tuning further.
