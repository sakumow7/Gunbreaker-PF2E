Gunbreaker

FFXIV-inspired PF2e Remaster class · Playtest 1.3.0

ClassFeatures & actionsFeatsGunblades

Gunnhildr's blades protect their companions with aetheric wards, then unleash explosive sword combinations. This PF2e Remaster playtest adapts the FFXIV job's identity to three-action turns.

Initial Proficiencies

Key attribute Strength or Dexterity; 10 + Constitution modifier HP per level. Trained Perception, expert Fortitude, trained Reflex and Will. Trained simple weapons, martial weapons, unarmed attacks, light and medium armor, unarmored defense, and Gunbreaker class DC. Trained Athletics plus 3 + Intelligence modifier additional skills. Use the d6 finesse Light Gunblade for a Dexterity build, or the d8/two-hand d12 Gunblade for Strength.

You gain standard attribute boosts at levels 1, 5, 10, 15, and 20, ancestry feats at 1/5/9/13/17, class feats at 1 and every even level, skill feats at every even level, general feats at 3/7/11/15/19, and skill increases at every odd level starting at 3. Skill proficiency is capped at expert before level 7 and master before level 15.

During exploration, your alertness and Athletics help you escort companions and clear dangerous routes. Outside battle, you may be a bodyguard, weaponsmith, or keeper of Gunnhildr's traditions. There are no mandatory religious edicts.

Features & actions

Brutal Shell — Level 1

1 action(s) · gunbreaker, magical

Requirements Your Keen Edge combo opening.

Make a melee gunblade Strike. On a hit, regain Hit Points equal to half your level (minimum 1), and gain that many temporary Hit Points until the end of your next turn. This healing and barrier can occur only once per round. After resolving the Strike, consume the opening and enable Solid Barrel until the end of your next turn.

Burst Strike — Level 1

1 action(s) · gunbreaker, magical

Cost 1 cartridge.

Make a melee gunblade Strike. On a hit, deal additional fire damage: 1d6 at 1st level, 2d6 at 5th, 3d6 at 11th, and 4d6 at 17th. If you have Continuation, a hit enables Hypervelocity as your next action this turn against the same target.

Additional Strike damage: (1+floor((@actor.level+1)/6))d6[fire]

Demon Slaughter — Level 1

2 action(s) · gunbreaker

Requirements Your Demon Slice combo opening. Sweep your gunblade through a 5-foot emanation. Each enemy in the area attempts a basic Reflex save against your Gunbreaker class DC. Roll the damage once: 1d4 slashing damage per 2 levels, rounded up. This is not a Strike; do not add weapon damage, runes, Strength, or specialization. This activity does not use or increase multiple attack penalty.

After resolving the saves, end the combo and load 1 cartridge, even if all targets succeed. At least one enemy must be in the area.

Gunbreaker's Combo — Level 1

passive · gunbreaker

You learn Keen Edge, Brutal Shell, Solid Barrel, Burst Strike, Demon Slice, and Demon Slaughter at 1st level. All these actions require you to wield a gunblade. Combo actions can be used only in a combat encounter with a genuine threat.

You can maintain one combo sequence at a time. Keen Edge opens Brutal Shell, which opens Solid Barrel. Demon Slice opens Demon Slaughter. Each step consumes the previous opening and enables the next until the end of your next turn. Advance the sequence after resolving the action, even on a miss. Starting another sequence replaces the old one. Other actions, including movement, defense, normal Strikes, and cartridge spenders that do not start a sequence, do not interrupt it.

All Strikes use and increase multiple attack penalty normally. Bonus damage belongs to the Strike and doubles on a critical hit unless specified otherwise. Count only the base weapon damage die and fundamental striking rune dice when a feature refers to weapon damage dice; extra dice from abilities or property runes do not count. No Mercy modifies gunblade Strike damage, not separate save-based blasts or Continuation damage.

Heart Of Stone — Level 1

reaction · gunbreaker, magical

Trigger An ally other than you within 15 feet would take damage from an enemy's Strike. Requirements You wield a gunblade and can see the ally and attacker.

You project a protective ward. The ally gains resistance to all damage from the triggering Strike equal to 2 + half your level (rounded down). Before applying the resistance, you can spend 1 cartridge to increase it to 2 + your level. This is resistance, not additional damage reduction; overlapping resistances use the highest applicable value. This does not move either creature or change the target of the attack.

Keen Edge — Level 1

1 action(s) · gunbreaker

Make a melee gunblade Strike against an enemy. After resolving it, begin the single-target combo, enabling Brutal Shell until the end of your next turn.

Powder Gauge — Level 1

passive · gunbreaker

Your gunblade stores aether in a Powder Gauge with a maximum of 3 cartridges. At the start of a combat encounter, begin with 1 cartridge; unspent cartridges dissipate at its end. You cannot build cartridges outside a combat encounter with a genuine threat, as determined by the GM. A training target or cooperative creature is not a threat.

Completing Solid Barrel or Demon Slaughter loads 1 cartridge, even if the Strike misses or every target succeeds at its save. Each activity loads only once, regardless of targets. You cannot exceed your maximum. Costs are paid before resolving an action and are not refunded on a miss. Cartridges are class resources, not ammunition or trade goods. Round fractional values down, to a minimum of 1 only where stated.

Use the Gunbreaker Tracker macro to record cartridges, combos, and cooldowns. Posting an item to chat never spends or grants resources. Effects, attacks, saves, damage, healing, and reactions are resolved on the sheet with your GM.

Unless a feature says otherwise, you must wield a gunblade to use Gunbreaker actions and reactions. Passive features do not require this. Aurora and defensive abilities without an encounter requirement may be used during exploration; their cooldowns still apply.

Royal Guard — Level 1

1 action(s) · gunbreaker, stance

Requirements You wield a gunblade.

You adopt the protective posture of Gunnhildr's guard. Gain a +1 circumstance bonus to AC while in this stance. Your damage is unchanged. The stance ends if you cease wielding a gunblade, become unconscious, or combat ends. Your Heart of Stone reaction works with or without the stance. This stance does not force enemies to attack you.

Apply the linked Royal Guard effect when you enter, and remove it when you leave.

Effect: Royal Guard (Stance). Apply manually after recording the action.

Continuation — Level 7

passive · gunbreaker

You learn Hypervelocity, Jugular Rip, Abdomen Tear, and Eye Gouge. After a successful qualifying melee Strike, you may use its follow-up as your next action on the same turn: Burst Strike → Hypervelocity; Gnashing Fang → Jugular Rip; Savage Claw → Abdomen Tear; Wicked Talon → Eye Gouge. A miss grants no follow-up. Any intervening action or the end of your turn closes the opening.

You may use only one Continuation follow-up per round, including Fated Brand. It targets the same creature, which must remain within your gunblade's melee reach. The follow-up requires no additional attack roll, does not affect multiple attack penalty, and is a separate damage instance for resistance and weakness. It never doubles because the opening Strike critically hit. These limits also apply to follow-ups granted before this feature.

On a hit, deal 2d6 fire damage to up to two other enemies that are both within your melee reach and adjacent to the primary target; each attempts a basic Reflex save against your class DC. The secondary damage does not depend on the primary Strike's critical result.

After resolving the Strike, enable Noble Blood until the end of your next turn. You may use only one Reign sequence action per turn. Strikes use and increase multiple attack penalty normally. The sequence does not grant Continuation.

Additional Strike damage 2d6[slashing]; secondary 2d6[fire] — basic Reflex save against your Gunbreaker class DC

Relentless Rush — Level 18

2 action(s) · gunbreaker, magical

Frequency once per 10 minutes. Requirements You wield a gunblade. Enemies in a 5-foot emanation take 8d6 fire damage with a basic Reflex save against your class DC. Gain resistance to all damage equal to half your level until the start of your next turn. At the start of your next turn, enemies then in a 5-foot emanation take 4d6 fire damage with a new basic Reflex save against your class DC. The second blast occurs only if you remain conscious and wielding a gunblade. Neither blast uses or increases multiple attack penalty.

8d6[fire], then 4d6[fire] — basic Reflex save against your Gunbreaker class DC

Gunmetal Soul — Level 20

2 action(s) · gunbreaker, magical

Frequency once per day. Requirements You can act and wield a gunblade. You and allies currently within 30 feet gain resistance to all damage equal to your level until the start of your next turn. Recipients retain the benefit if they leave the area. This does not stack with other resistance to the same damage. You cannot activate it while unconscious or otherwise unable to take its two actions.

Perfect Chamber — Level 20

1 action(s) · gunbreaker, magical

Frequency once per day. Requirements You wield a gunblade. For 1 minute or until the encounter ends, whichever comes first, reduce the cartridge cost of the first cartridge-spending action or reaction you use each round by 1 (minimum 0). You choose whether to apply the reduction when spending. This includes the optional cartridge enhancement to Heart of Stone. Costs otherwise apply normally, and cooldowns and combo requirements are unchanged. This does not increase cartridge generation.

Gunblades

Gunblade — Level 0

two-hand-d12, gunbreaker

A one-handed martial sword with an aetheric firing chamber: 1d8 slashing, two-hand d12, sword group, Bulk 1. Gunbreaker actions discharge its cartridges; it is not a firearm or combination weapon and has no separate ammunition, reload, or ranged Strike without a class ability. Fire damage comes from abilities or runes, not a versatile trait.

Light Gunblade — Level 0

finesse, gunbreaker

A one-handed martial sword with a lighter aetheric chamber: 1d6 slashing, finesse, sword group, Bulk 1, price 10 gp. It qualifies as a gunblade for all class abilities. It has no two-hand trait. Use Dexterity for melee attack rolls if desired; Strength still modifies melee damage normally. Its cartridges function identically to those of a standard gunblade.
